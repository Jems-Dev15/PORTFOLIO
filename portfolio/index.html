About me

A little about who I am and how I like to work.

I'm an aspiring full-stack developer who enjoys turning everyday problems into working software. I build web applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, and I also work with Kotlin, Java, XML, SQLite and Firebase for Android projects. I write C, C++ and C# when a project calls for it.

Most of my projects start with a practical need: keeping track of employees, stock, expenses or student records, whether through a web application or a mobile app. I like the whole path, from designing the database to building the screen someone actually uses.

I'm focused on writing cleaner code, getting more comfortable with frameworks like Laravel, and finishing projects that real people can use. I'm always learning new technologies and open to opportunities and collaborations.
